eQaida: teacher test

Takhti 1–3 · interactive version · please try it with a few students and send us your notes

▶ Open eQaida

Or open the eQaida link directly.

1. Before you start

  • Open the link in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (laptop, tablet or phone). It needs internet.
  • Nothing to download or install. Bookmark the link to find it again.
  • For an online class, share your screen. For a child beside you, a touch screen or smartboard is best.

2. How to use it (2 minutes)

  • It looks like the printed Qaida. Turn pages with the ‹ › buttons at the top or the ← → keys.
  • Every box has a small gold eye button. Tap it to open the help for that box. Help buttons (top) hides or shows them.
  • In a pop-up: Next box / Previous box moves through the table. Esc or ✕ closes it.
Page 3 (the letters): three short steps; the teacher taps Next step →
  1. Meet it: the big letter, its name and one memory line (e.g. ب “a boat with 1 dot under it”).
  2. Trace it: the child traces the letter. The bar shows % done; only ink on the letter counts and every dot must be traced. When it is complete: MashaAllah! with confetti. Clear starts again.
  3. Find it: “Which one is Ba?” The child taps the right letter among look-alikes (ب ت ث).
New: practice and games (every page). Tap the green 🎯 Practice this page button at the top.
  1. Choose what to practise: tap the letters taught so far (or use Row 1, Rows 1–2 …).
  2. Choose an activity, or tap ▶ Quick practice (about 5 minutes: Flash cards → Find it → Balloon pop).
  • Flash cards: the child says it, you tap ✓ or ✗. Missed cards come back.
  • Find it: you say the name, the child taps the right one.
  • Balloon pop and Memory match: games for the younger children.
  • Spot it (pages with words): the child finds a letter inside a joined word.
  • Read check: the page in shuffled order. Tap the boxes the child read wrong, then Done. Use this to check memorizing.
  • 📊 Progress: which letters the child knows (green), is learning (yellow) or needs practice (red).
  • Tap 👤 Student 1 (top right) and add each student's name, so every child keeps their own progress. Tick Age 3–5 for the little ones.
  • Online class: share your screen. Every choice has a number. The child says the number and you tap it, or press that number key on your keyboard. (Untick Online class if the child is beside you and taps the screen.)
  • Sound effects (balloon pops, clapping, party poppers): so the child hears them, turn on sound when you share your screen. Zoom: tick Share sound. Google Meet: share a Chrome tab and keep Also share tab audio on. Teams: turn on Include computer sound. The 🔊 button at the top turns the sounds off or on.
Pages 4–6 (Makharij): a realistic 3D head shows the tongue or lips moving to the makhraj, with the air flow and a short explanation below. Some letters have more views: Side view, Front view (e.g. the tongue between the teeth for ث ذ ظ, the lips for ب م ف و), or Roof of the mouth (ل ن ر). Buttons: Pause, Again, Slow, Labels on/off.
Later pages: pages 9A–9D show the Start / Middle / End shapes of each letter; the compound pages break a word into its letters; the Fatha / Kasra / Damma pages show each sound.

3. What we would like you to check

  1. Do the children learn and remember the letters faster with Meet it → Trace it → Find it?
  2. Is tracing easy with a finger, mouse or pen? Does the % done feel fair? Is the pen too thick or too thin?
  3. Are the tongue and lip movements correct for each makhraj? Please check every letter with your own knowledge.
  4. Are the memory lines good? How do you describe the letters to children?
  5. Which practice activities do the children enjoy, and which really help them remember? Is anything missing?
  6. Does the Progress map match what the child really knows?
  7. Was anything confusing, too fast, too small or did anything not work?
